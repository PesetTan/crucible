# Gauntlet solid cuff revision

Built-in image generation edits replace the former anvil cavity with a structural faceted wrist plate.

## silver

Use case: precise-object-edit. Fix the empty cuff in this Gauntlet silver logo. The large dark hollow in the LOWER WRIST, where the anvil was removed, is structurally wrong. FILL THAT ENTIRE HOLLOW with a solid continuous armored WRIST PLATE in the SAME silver metal as the rest of this gauntlet. This plate is integrated into the cuff, connected naturally below the main back-of-hand armor, with a raised central ridge and 2 or 3 broad angular facets following the wrist contour. Match existing facet shading and edge highlights. It must read as actual armor covering a wrist, no hole, no inset symbol area, no black void, no floating badge. Keep only THIN dark seam lines around the new plate, consistent with existing armor joints. Preserve all fingers, thumb, upper palm plates, outer cuff silhouette, exact chosen monochrome color palette, orientation, size and composition. Keep navy background and existing white Gauntlet wordmark and top color label. NO ANVIL, foundry, forge symbol, ornaments, gems or replacement emblem. Main requested change is a believable solid faceted wrist/cuff center.
